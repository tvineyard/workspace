Roster data

All 37 seasons from 1990 through 2026 are bundled into this page. Nothing is fetched while you play, so the game works offline and every season is always available.

Rosters come from the University of Tennessee Athletics roster archive at utsports.com, cross-checked against the public CollegeFootballData dataset. Coaches and support staff are excluded. Historical rosters do not change, so this data is fixed.

The search list deliberately shows names only - not seasons - so it doesn't reveal whether a suggestion is correct for the year on screen.
