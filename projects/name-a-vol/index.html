How it works

Name anyone who was on that season's roster. You get three guesses, and small spelling mistakes are fine — a last name on its own counts too.

Once you've used a player, he's spent for the rest of your run. Miss three times and the streak resets.

Football covers 1990–2026, basketball 1990–91 through 2025–26. Rosters come from the University of Tennessee Athletics archive.
