How it works

Classic — name anyone on that season's roster. By position — only players at the position named count. How many — name as many as you can at that position before three wrong guesses.

Small spelling mistakes are fine, and a last name on its own counts. In Classic and By position, a player you've used is spent for the rest of your run, and three misses resets the streak.

Football covers 1990–2026, basketball 1990–91 through 2025–26. Rosters come from the University of Tennessee Athletics archive.
